---
title: "Automated inboxes"
description: "Let a workflow process every record that enters an inbox, and send only the exceptions to your team."
---

An **automated inbox** gives its records to a workflow instead of your operators. Each record that enters the inbox's segment starts the workflow, and your team only steps in when a run needs a person.

For example, every document that enters the `Waiting for validation` segment starts a workflow that checks it and sets its status to `Validated`.

## How it works

An automation uses two inboxes:

- **The automated inbox** receives every record of the segment. The workflow processes them, nobody takes them.
- **The fallback inbox** receives a task when a run needs a person: an error, a confirmation or a manual step. Operators work it like any [inbox](/product/manage/inbox), and the run continues where it stopped.

A record is done when it leaves the segment. End your workflow with a step that changes what the segment filters on, for instance an **Update Data** step that sets the status to `Validated`.

New records start within 5 minutes.

## Before you start

1. Create a segment with the records the workflow should process.
2. Create a workflow on the same collection. In its **Triggers** section, pick a **Fallback inbox** and turn on **On data change**. See [Workflow triggers](/product/process/workflows/triggers#the-on-data-change-trigger).
3. Add a service account to the inbox's team. The workflow runs with its permissions.
4. Ask your developers to run [Forest Runtime](/product/process/workflows/forest-runtime) on your environment.

## Set up an automated inbox

Only Admins can pick the service account in **Runs as**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="New inbox">
    1. Turn on **Layout Editor** mode, click **Add new** at the bottom of the left navbar, then pick **Inbox**.
    2. Name the inbox, then pick its **Collection** and **Segment**.
    3. Pick the dispatch rule. The workflow starts the records in this order.
    4. Pick **A workflow processes them**, then the **Workflow** and the service account in **Runs as**.
    5. Click **Confirm**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Existing inbox">
    1. Turn on **Layout Editor** mode and click the inbox in the left navbar.
    2. In the **Process** section, set **How it starts** to **Automatically, as soon as a record enters**.
    3. Pick the **Workflow** and the service account in **Runs as**, then click **Save**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The workflow also starts on the records already in the segment. Forest shows how many before you confirm.

## Follow the automation

Admins open an automated inbox from the left navbar, where a bolt icon marks it. It opens on its **Backlog**:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Doing** | The workflow is processing the record. |
| **Escalated** | The run waits on a person in the fallback inbox. Click the fallback inbox name in the header to reach it. |

A record leaves the Backlog once it leaves the segment. Click **Refresh** to update the list.

## Pause or stop the automation

To pause, open the inbox settings in **Layout Editor** mode and click **Pause** in the **Process** section. No new run starts, and the runs already going finish. Click **Resume** to start again.

To stop, choose **Operators know what to do** in the **Process** section and click **Save**. Operators work the inbox again.
