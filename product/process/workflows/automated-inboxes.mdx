A workflow can start on its own each time a record enters a segment. Your team only steps in when a run needs a person.

For example, every document that enters the `Waiting for validation` segment starts a workflow that checks it and sets its status to `Validated`.

## How it works

The workflow uses two inboxes:

- **The automated inbox** lists every record of the segment. The workflow processes them, nobody takes them.
- **The fallback inbox** receives a task when a run needs a person: an error, a confirmation or a manual step. Operators work it like any [inbox](/product/manage/inbox), and the run continues where it stopped.

A record is done when it leaves the segment. End your workflow with a step that changes what the segment filters on, for instance an **Update Data** step that sets the status to `Validated`.

New records start within 5 minutes by default. Your developers can change this interval in [Forest Runtime](/product/process/workflows/forest-runtime#tuning).

## Before you start

To start a workflow on data change, you need:

- A segment on the workflow's collection.
- A **Fallback inbox** and the **On data change** trigger turned on, in the workflow's **Triggers** section. See [Workflow triggers](/product/process/workflows/triggers#the-on-data-change-trigger).
- A service account in the inbox's team. The workflow runs with its permissions.
- [Forest Runtime](/product/process/workflows/forest-runtime) running on your environment.

## Set it up

Only Admins and Process Owners can pick the service account in **Runs as**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the workflow">
    1. In the workflow's **Triggers** section, under **On data change**, click **New automatic inbox**.
    2. Pick the **Segment**.
    3. Pick the dispatch rule. The workflow starts the records in this order.
    4. Pick the service account in **Runs as**.
    5. Click **Confirm**.

    You can also create the inbox from the Customization Center: **Add new** → **Inbox**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From an existing inbox">
    1. Turn on **Layout Editor** mode and click the inbox in the left navbar.
    2. In the **Process** section, set **How it starts** to **Automatically, as soon as a record enters**.
    3. Pick the **Workflow** and the service account in **Runs as**, then click **Save**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The workflow also starts on the records already in the segment. Forest shows how many before you confirm.

## Follow the runs

The workflow's **On data change** row lists its automated inboxes, with their state and service account.

Admins open an automated inbox from the left navbar, where a bolt icon marks it. It opens on its **Backlog**:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Doing** | The workflow is processing the record. |
| **Escalated** | The run waits on a person in the fallback inbox. Click the fallback inbox name in the header to reach it. |

A record leaves the Backlog once it leaves the segment. Click **Refresh** to update the list.

## Pause or stop

To pause, open the inbox settings in **Layout Editor** mode and click **Pause** in the **Process** section. No new run starts, and the runs already going finish. Click **Resume** to start again.

To stop for one inbox, choose **Operators know what to do** in its **Process** section and click **Save**. Operators work the inbox again.

To stop for every inbox, turn off **On data change** in the workflow's **Triggers** section.
